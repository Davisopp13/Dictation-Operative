How to use this test runner

Keep the workspace open in another tab, or open the Mac app for its tests. Do the action shown, compare it with the expected result, and select Worked, Problem, or Skip. Then choose Next step.

Select a numbered circle to revisit a step. Use All steps to explore a feature or the Problems counter to review failures. Skipped steps still need testing; they do not count as passed.

When you finish, choose Copy report and paste it into your development task. Your report includes results from every area.
